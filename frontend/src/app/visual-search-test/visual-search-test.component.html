Visual search test

In each iteration, you will see multiple figures in a grid. Your task is to click as quickly as possible on the upright red letter T.
The other figures (blue or rotated) serve as distractors.
If you do not see an upright red letter T, right-click anywhere on the screen.

Click the blue button below to start the test.

Thank you for your time! In the task you have just completed, you were asked to find the target and respond to it while distractions were present around it. Such tasks are used in the psychology of attention to study how people direct their focus and how difficult it is for them to ignore irrelevant information. What you may not know is that these tests are not used only in laboratories, but also in applied fields—for example, in the assessment of drivers, pilots, and people working in jobs where every second counts. During the task, your reaction time, the way you moved the mouse, and the speed with which you recognized the target were measured. All of this information together helps researchers better understand how attentional and decision-making processes unfold.
